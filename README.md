The joke is a setup-and-twist delivered by a dad at the dinner table: a lamp that can't tell jokes because every time it tries to lighten the mood, someone switches it off before the punchline.

> I asked my lamp why it never tells jokes — it said every time it tries to lighten the mood, someone switches it off before the punchline.
